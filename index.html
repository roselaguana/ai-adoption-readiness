About this instrument

What it is. A readiness diagnostic I designed to make a hard question concrete: is an organization ready to adopt AI agents, or will the rollout stall? The six dimensions map to established change-management thinking (sponsorship, capability, reinforcement) applied to the specific problem of deploying autonomous and semi-autonomous AI.

What the demo data is. The example companies are fictional, illustrative profiles — built to show how the scoring behaves across different maturity shapes, not records of real engagements.

Why I built it. I spent 27 years running operations on the road for live productions — the work of getting people, systems, and a plan to actually hold up under pressure. This tool applies that operator's lens to AI adoption. The scoring logic is deterministic and runs entirely in your browser; there is no backend and nothing is collected.
